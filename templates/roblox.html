What is Roblox? -Online platform and game creation system developed by Roblox Corporation. Launched in 2006, but exploded in popularity during the 2020s. Hosts millions of user-created games (“experiences”) across many genres. Free to play on PC, console, and mobile devices. How It Works Players create an account, customize their avatar, and explore thousands of worlds. Experiences are built by users using Roblox Studio, a powerful creation tool. Many games are free; some use Robux, Roblox’s virtual currency. Popular Types of Experiences Adventure games (Obbys, story games) Role-play simulations (Brookhaven, Bloxburg) Survival and horror games (DOORS, Rainbow Friends) Competitive games (Arsenal, BedWars) Tycoons and simulators Social hangout worlds Key Features Massive user-generated content library—new games appear daily. Cross-platform play across all major devices. Avatar customization with outfits, accessories, animations, and more. Developers can earn real money through the Roblox Developer Exchange (DevEx). Built-in chat, friends, and private servers for social play. Why It’s Popular Endless variety—a platform, not just a game. Easy for anyone (including kids and teens) to jump in and play. Huge community of creators and players. Regular updates, events, and brand collaborations. Encourages creativity through coding, building, and game design.
