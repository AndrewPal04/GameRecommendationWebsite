1. Game Modes

-Campaign: A story mode where you follow soldiers through missions around the world. The focus is on teamwork, gadgets, and completing objectives. (No graphic details.)
-Multiplayer: Compete online against other players in quick, strategic matches. You level up, unlock gear, and learn different playstyles.
-Zombies: Team up to survive waves of zombie enemies in a more fantasy/scifi setting.
-Spec Ops / Missions: Challenge-style missions you complete with teammates.
-Warzone (in some games): A large-scale mode where many players fight to be the last team standing.

2. The World

-Maps vary a lot—cities, deserts, snow bases, jungles, high-tech facilities, and more.
-You use different equipment depending on the game: drones, gadgets, tactical tools, etc.
-Each new title usually introduces new maps, weapons, characters, and abilities.

3. Progression

-Earn XP to unlock new gear, custom weapon setups, and perks.
-Learn map layouts, strategies, and team tactics. -Some games have special storylines that continue across multiple titles.

4. Creativity & Community

-Players customize loadouts, operators (characters), weapon skins, and calling cards.
-The community runs competitive matches, clans, tournaments, and special in-game events.
-Some titles allow player-made modes or custom games.
