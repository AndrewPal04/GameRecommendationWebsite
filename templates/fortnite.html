1. What is Fortnite?

-Online multiplayer game developed by Epic Games (released in 2017).
-Famous for its Battle Royale, Zero Build, and Creative modes.
-Free-to-play and available on PC, consoles, and mobile (via cloud services).

2. Game Modes

-Battle Royale
-100 players drop onto an island.
-Gather weapons, build structures, and fight to be the last player or team standing.
-Regular map updates and seasonal events keep gameplay fresh.
-Zero Build
-No building structures-focuses on movement, aim, and strategy.
-Fast-paced and popular with competitive players.
-Creative Mode
-Players design their own maps, games, and experiences.
-Used heavily by creators and community map designers.
-LEGO Fortnite
-Sandbox survival adventure inspired by LEGO mechanics.
-Build villages, craft items, and explore procedurally-generated worlds.

3. Key Features

-Seasonal updates with new themes, map changes, and rewards.
-Cross-platform play across all major systems.
-Cosmetics-driven progression (skins, emotes, pickaxes-no pay-to-win items).
-Frequent collaborations with Marvel, Star Wars, anime, musicians, and more.
-Massive in-game events, concerts, and story finales.

4. Why It's Popular

-Constant updates and variety keep gameplay fresh.
-Easy to start, hard to master-appeals to casual and competitive players.
-Strong social features and global community.
Creative content allows limitless player-made experiences.
