— Environments can change during matches, affecting strategy

— Learning how heroes work together is more important than raw aim
— Players unlock cosmetic items and customization as they play

— The game encourages creative team combinations and strategies
— It appeals to both Marvel fans and competitive players
— Regular updates and new heroes keep gameplay fresh
