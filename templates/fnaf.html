You got recommended FNAF!!


1. Game Modes / Gameplay

-You play as a night security guard watching over a building.
-The goal is to survive from midnight to morning by monitoring cameras, lights, doors, or systems.
-You must carefully manage power and tools—using them too much can leave you defenseless.

2. The Setting

-The games mostly take place in closed locations like pizza restaurants or facilities.
-Animatronic characters move around the building when you’re not watching them.
-The atmosphere is dark and tense, designed to make players feel uneasy (without graphic content).

3. Progression

-Each night gets harder as the animatronics become more active.
-Later games add new mechanics, characters, and systems to manage.
-Players slowly uncover story details through clues, mini-games, and hidden messages.

4. Story & Community

-The series has a deep, mysterious story told indirectly, encouraging theories and discussion.
-Fans create artwork, videos, theories, and custom games.
FNAF has expanded into books, spin-offs, and a movie.
