1. Game Modes / Gameplay

Smash Battles: Up to four (or more) players fight at once.
Attacks increase an opponent’s damage percentage, making them easier to launch.
Matches can be played casually with items or competitively with fewer rules.

2. The World

Stages are based on famous locations from Nintendo and other game series.
Each stage has platforms, hazards, and unique layouts.
Music and visuals celebrate gaming history.

3. Characters

A large roster of fighters from different franchises, each with unique moves.
Characters play very differently, encouraging many playstyles.
New characters are added in newer games, expanding the crossover.

4. Progression & Community

Players unlock characters, stages, and modes in some entries.
The game is popular at parties, tournaments, and esports events.
Super Smash Bros. has a huge competitive and casual community.
