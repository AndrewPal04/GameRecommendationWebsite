1. Game Modes / Gameplay

You control a cube (and other forms later) that moves automatically.
Tapping makes your character jump, fly, flip gravity, or change modes.
One mistake restarts the level, so timing and practice are key.

2. The World

Levels are made up of spikes, platforms, portals, and moving hazards.
Each level is synced to energetic music that helps guide timing.
Visual effects and colors change as levels progress.

3. Progression

Levels increase in difficulty, from easy to extremely challenging.
Players unlock new icons, colors, and effects.
Coins and achievements reward exploration and skill.

4. Creativity & Community

A powerful level editor lets players create and share custom levels.
The community makes everything from beginner levels to extreme challenges.
Geometry Dash has a huge fan base, competitions, and long-lasting popularity.
