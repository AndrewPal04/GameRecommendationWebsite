1. Game Modes

-Unrated: Standard matches where teams play without affecting rank.
-Competitive: Ranked matches where players climb tiers based on performance and teamwork.
-Spike Rush: A shorter, faster version of the main mode.
-Deathmatch & Practice Modes: Used to improve aim and learn weapons.

2. The World

-Matches take place on carefully designed maps with lanes, choke points, and objectives.
-Two teams of five take turns attacking and defending.
-Attackers try to plant the Spike, while defenders try to stop it or defuse it.

3. Agents & Abilities

-Players choose Agents, each with unique abilities.
-Agents fall into roles like Duelists, Controllers, Sentinels, and Initiators.
-Abilities support gunplay—they help control space, gather information, or protect teammates.

4. Progression & Community

-Players improve by learning maps, mastering aim, and coordinating with their team.
-You can unlock cosmetic items like skins, sprays, and player cards.
-Valorant has a large esports scene, regular updates, and an active competitive community.
