1. Game Modes / Gameplay

-You control a character who runs automatically forward.
-Swipe to jump, roll, or switch lanes to avoid trains and barriers.
-The game ends when you crash, but you can restart and try again.

2. The World

-The tracks are set in subway systems inspired by real cities.
-New locations rotate regularly, changing the look and theme of the game.
-Bright colors and smooth animations keep the game fast and fun.

3. Progression

-Collect coins to unlock new characters, boards, and upgrades.
-Power-ups like jetpacks, magnets, and score boosters help you run farther.
-Daily challenges and missions give extra rewards.

4. Creativity & Community

-Players customize characters and hoverboards with different styles.
-The game has events, seasonal updates, and leaderboards.
-It’s one of the most popular mobile games worldwide because it’s easy to learn and hard to master.
