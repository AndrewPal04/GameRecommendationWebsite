1. Game Modes / Gameplay

-Campaign: Play through a story with missions that mix combat, exploration, and vehicles.
-Multiplayer: Compete online or locally in team-based matches with different objectives.
-Co-op & Custom Games: Play missions or create matches with friends.

2. The World

-Set in a futuristic universe with alien structures and space environments.
-Players explore planets, space stations, and massive ring-shaped worlds called Halos.
-Vehicles like tanks and aircraft are a big part of gameplay.

3. Progression

-Players unlock cosmetics, ranks, and achievements.
-Skill comes from learning maps, weapon placements, and teamwork.
-Campaigns often continue a larger story across multiple games.

4. Story & Community

-The story centers on heroism, survival, and protecting humanity.
-Halo has a long-standing fanbase with competitive play, esports, and creative custom modes.
-It's known for its music, memorable characters, and balanced multiplayer.
