1. Game Modes / Gameplay

-Players drive cars that can boost, jump, and flip.
-Matches are usually 1v1, 2v2, or 3v3.
-Timing, positioning, and teamwork are more important than raw speed.

2. The World

-Games take place in enclosed arenas with different themes.
-Walls and ceilings can be driven on, allowing aerial plays.
-The arenas are designed to keep matches fast and fair.

3. Progression

-Players earn experience to level up.
-Ranked modes place players into skill tiers.
-Improving mechanics like aerials, dribbling, and rotations helps you climb ranks.

4. Customization & Community

-Cars can be customized with decals, wheels, boosts, and goal explosions.
-The game has a strong competitive and esports community.
-Regular updates, events, and new modes keep the game fresh.
